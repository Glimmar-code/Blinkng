Request deletion of your BLINK account

You can ask to delete your account and the personal data associated with it. This request path is also available if you no longer have BLINK installed.

How to request deletion

  1. If signed in, open BLINK → Settings and privacy → Delete account.
  2. Alternatively, email BLINK Support from your account email address. Put “BLINK account deletion request” in the subject.
  3. BLINK Support may verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request. You should never send your password, verification codes or identity documents in your first request.

Request account deletion by email

What will be deleted?

Account profile information and associated personal content will be removed or de-identified where applicable when the request is fulfilled. Certain transaction, security or legal records may be retained where legally required; our Privacy Policy describes these cases. Deleting your BLINK account does not necessarily delete content another person has independently shared.

Important: This page provides a request route, not an instant automated deletion service. BLINK must verify inbox handling, account ownership and end-to-end deletion before relying on this URL for a Play submission.
