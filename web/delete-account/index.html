BLINK account controls

Delete your BLINK account

This page is the public web resource for BLINK account-deletion requests. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from the Android app

  1. Sign in to BLINK.
  2. Open Settings and privacy.
  3. Open Login & account security, then choose Delete account.
  4. Review the confirmation notice and confirm the deletion request.

Delete from the web

Sign in at blink.com.ng/login, open your account settings, and use the account-deletion control. If the web control is not available for your account, use the in-app control above. Do not send passwords, payment details, or recovery codes through a support message.

What happens to your data

BLINK deletes the account and associated user data through the authenticated account-deletion workflow, including profile and service records that are eligible for deletion. Some limited security, fraud-prevention, transaction, or legal records may be retained only where necessary and permitted by law, as described in the BLINK Privacy Policy. Public posts or marketplace records may be removed or anonymized as part of the deletion workflow.

Need help?

For a deletion problem, use the support/contact method displayed on the official BLINK website or the in-app support controls. BLINK will verify account ownership before handling a deletion request.
