Using BLINK safely

Protect your account, make thoughtful choices about what you share and use extra care when dealing with people or product listings online.

Protect your BLINK account

Use a unique password and keep sign-in codes and recovery messages private. Only enter your BLINK credentials on the official website or app. If you receive an unexpected sign-in request, do not forward your codes to anyone.

BLINK's public pages can be read without logging in, but some interactions require an account. Never share a password with someone claiming to be support.

Choose what to share

Public posts, profiles and reels can be seen by other people. Avoid posting private addresses, access credentials or personal information about someone else without their permission.

Use the privacy options available to your account and read the BLINK Privacy Policy for more details about information processing.

Take care in marketplace conversations

Treat unfamiliar listings carefully. Compare descriptions, inspect items when possible and use well-understood payment arrangements. Do not assume a seller badge guarantees the outcome of a purchase.

If a transaction or conversation makes you uncomfortable, stop and use relevant reporting or support channels when available.

Explore BLINK

For an overview of supported capabilities, read the BLINK features page. You can also download the official Android app or browse BLINK in a web browser.
