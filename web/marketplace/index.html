Discover BLINK Market

BLINK Market brings product discovery into the BLINK community. Marketplace options and seller capabilities may depend on your account and app version.

Browse products and discover sellers

BLINK Market is designed to help people explore product listings and discover sellers within the BLINK community. Items, availability, prices and seller participation can change.

Product listings are separate from regular social posts. Visit BLINK and open Market to see the options available to your account.

Selling requires approval

BLINK's planned marketplace rules reserve listing creation for approved sellers who meet account and verification requirements. Approval and active feature availability must be confirmed in the app; a verified badge by itself does not guarantee a transaction.

The website does not promise that BLINK processes purchases, guarantees delivery or provides a completed buyer-protection service. Confirm available payment and dispute options before transacting.

Shop responsibly

Check product descriptions and photos carefully. Ask reasonable questions, confirm the seller and inspect items safely before any payment where appropriate.

Be cautious with requests for unexpected deposits or personal information. See the BLINK safety page for more tips.
