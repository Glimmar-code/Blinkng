BLINK for students and campus communities in Nigeria

Explore how BLINK brings social sharing, discovery and campus connections into one experience. Availability of individual features depends on the platform and app version.

Discover people around your interests

A campus community is more than a directory. BLINK gives people a place to create profiles, follow others and explore public posts and reels. You can discover creators and conversations that matter to you.

BLINK provides a searchable catalogue of Nigerian universities on its public homepage. A university appearing in the catalogue does not mean a verified community or a particular number of students is active there.

Share your campus experience

Use your BLINK account to explore posts, reels and other supported social interactions. Before sharing, consider whether an update is suitable for a public audience and avoid revealing someone else's private details.

Account-based messaging can help you stay in touch, but feature availability varies between Android and the web.

Visit the features overview to see what BLINK offers and the official download page for the Android APK. Use official BLINK links rather than modified downloads.
