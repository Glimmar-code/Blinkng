About BLINK — a social and campus community app in Nigeria

BLINK brings people together online, with a focus on connection, expression and campus communities. Discover public profiles, explore posts and reels, follow creators and stay in touch with friends.

You can browse BLINK on the web or download the Android app. Your account connects your experience across supported BLINK platforms.
