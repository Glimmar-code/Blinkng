Frequently asked questions about BLINK

Find clear answers about the BLINK app in Nigeria, the official Android download, account access, and what you can do on BLINK.

What is BLINK Nigeria?

BLINK is a social and campus community app focused on connecting people, exploring profiles and sharing posts and reels. Learn more on the About page.

Where can I download the official BLINK Android app?

Use the Download page on www.blink.com.ng. It links to the latest published Android APK from the official BLINK GitHub Releases.

Can I use BLINK without downloading the Android app?

Yes. You can browse BLINK's public web experience at www.blink.com.ng. Sign in for the account features supported by the website.

How do I sign in to BLINK?

Open the BLINK website, choose Sign in to BLINK, and use your existing BLINK account details. The Android app also offers account access.

What can I do on BLINK?

Explore public profiles, posts and reels, connect with people and use messaging or other features available on your platform. Visit the Features page for an overview.

Is BLINK only for one university?

BLINK supports campus community connections, but its public website also lets people discover BLINK more broadly. Available campus features depend on the account and app version.

How do I know I am downloading the official BLINK app?

Use the official website at www.blink.com.ng and its Download page, which sends you to the project's GitHub Releases. Avoid unofficial links or altered APK files.

Where can I read BLINK's Privacy Policy?

The BLINK Privacy Policy is available at www.blink.com.ng/privacy/. It explains the categories of information BLINK may process and available privacy choices.

Ready to explore BLINK?

Go to the official site or download BLINK for Android from the project's published release channel.
