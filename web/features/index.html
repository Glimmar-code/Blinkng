Explore BLINK app features

BLINK brings public discovery, social sharing and personal connections together in one community-focused experience. Availability may vary by app version and device.

Connect, share and discover

Profiles and followers

Build a BLINK profile, connect with people and explore public accounts. Profile details help other people discover you.

Feed and posts

Share updates and explore content from your community, with supported social interactions.

Reels and video

Discover short-form video content and connect with people who post it.

Messages

Stay in touch through account-based messaging features on supported BLINK platforms.

Campus communities

Connect with fellow students and discover people relevant to your university or campus interests.

Creators and communities

Find public creator profiles and explore people, posts and communities on BLINK.

Marketplace and Connect

Access BLINK's marketplace and Connect areas where they are available to your account.

Android and web access

Use BLINK through the website or download the published Android app. Some capabilities are platform-specific.

Get started with BLINK

Discover BLINK without installing anything, or get the official Android app for your phone. Want more background? Read About BLINK Nigeria.
