BLINK brings campus communities together.

Explore posts and short videos, follow people you know, and discover conversations and opportunities across your university.

With BLINK you can:
• Create a profile and connect with other people.
• Share posts, photos and videos.
• Discover conversations and campus communities.
• Browse marketplace listings and connect with sellers.
• Manage notifications and privacy settings.

BLINK is also available on the web at blink.com.ng.

Some features require an account, network access or optional payments. Feature availability may vary by device and location.

Our goal is to help students discover, share and stay connected.
