# BLINK Google Play update readiness

BLINK is prepared now for Google Play distribution without requiring a Play Console account during development.

## Already implemented

- Production builds generate both a signed APK and a signed Android App Bundle (AAB).
- Release versionCode values are monotonically increasing.
- Production signing continuity is checked before release artifacts are produced.
- Release builds use code/resource shrinking.
- Android CI builds a debug AAB so bundle regressions are caught before merge.
- Google Play In-App Updates 2.1.0 is integrated with a flexible update flow.
- Sideload/GitHub APK installs remain supported. If Google Play update services are unavailable, BLINK starts normally and the Play update adapter is a no-op.
- After a flexible Play update downloads, BLINK offers a Restart action so Google Play can complete installation.

## Do not change before Play launch

- Keep applicationId: `com.aistudio.blink.appvtwo`.
- Keep the permanent production signing key and certificate continuity.
- Never reuse or decrease an Android versionCode.
- Continue producing release AAB files from the signed-release workflow.

## When a Google Play developer account is available

1. Create the BLINK app in Play Console with package `com.aistudio.blink.appvtwo`.
2. Enroll/configure Play App Signing carefully and preserve the existing upload/signing identity as required by Play Console.
3. Upload the signed AAB generated by the existing signed-release workflow to an internal testing track first.
4. Install that Play-distributed build on a test device.
5. Upload a newer AAB with a higher versionCode.
6. Verify Google Play reports an update and BLINK's flexible in-app update flow can start and complete it.
7. Only after internal testing succeeds, promote the release to production.

Google Play determines optimized/differential delivery. BLINK should not implement its own binary patcher for Play-distributed builds.
